[Holoboard](https://holoboard.space) is a board for finding Nostr notes. Lightning payments can raise a note's visibility, but the original notes remain Nostr events and Holoboard serves its ranking and appearance data from its own HTTP API.

## Promotion and clients

The [September 23 and 24 changelog](https://github.com/ptrio42/holoboard.space/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) records encrypted direct-message commands for requesting promotions and invoices, an opt-in expiry reminder, and an Android app. The [relay notes](https://github.com/ptrio42/holoboard.space/blob/main/relay/README.md) describe the Nostr post, comment, inbox, quote, and delete paths. The [signed Android listing](https://zapstore.dev/apps/space.holoboard.app) identifies the app's publisher; it does not by itself identify Holoboard's separate public board account.
