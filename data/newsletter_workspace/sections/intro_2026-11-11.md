This week [Marmot](/en/topics/marmot/), the encrypted-group messaging protocol, ran through the [bitcoin++ Seoul hackathon](/en/newsletters/2026-11-11-newsletter/#marmot-projects-at-bitcoin-seoul), where the winning project coordinated hardware signers over a Marmot group. [Nostur](/en/newsletters/2026-11-11-newsletter/#nostur) added polls, which this issue's [deep dive on NIP-88](/en/newsletters/2026-11-11-newsletter/#nip-deep-dive-nip-88-polls) (Nostr polls) explains. [Chama](/en/newsletters/2026-11-11-newsletter/#chama) changed how trades lock escrow, and [Route96](/en/newsletters/2026-11-11-newsletter/#route96) merged media-server security fixes. Elsewhere, relays gained a [rate-limit backoff hint](https://github.com/nostr-protocol/nips/pull/2498), and wallet-connection, Concord and Marmot specs moved.
